## Fun with Multiboard

I have been looking into how to take my hobby station to the next level and decided to give multiboard a try. 

I 3d printed and designed a multiboard grid from [multiboard.io](https://www.multiboard.io/) once assembled i 3d printed custom multiboard hooks to hang from my old skadis peg poard and set it all up on my hobby desk.
