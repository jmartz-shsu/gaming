## 40K League CSM vs Dark Angels - 1st Battle
I played my first game in the 40k League using my Chaos Space Marines (CSM) list to refresh my memory on the 10th edition rules. It was a 1,000-point match, and while my performance was decent, the game was a blast! My opponent, John, played Dark Angels, making for an exciting match. I’m already looking forward to my next game!
